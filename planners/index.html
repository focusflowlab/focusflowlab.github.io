Questions people ask

What do I get?

Two printable PDFs, A4 and US Letter, of 9 pages each: an exam map, a week-by-week overview, a weekly page, a daily focus page with four focus blocks and a self-test after each, a practice test log, an error log and a test-day checklist. Print as many copies of the weekly and daily pages as you need.

How do I receive it?

Right after payment you get a download page, and a receipt with the download link is emailed to you. Nothing is shipped.

Is this an official plan from the exam owner?

No. These are study planners made by FocusFlow Lab and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any exam owner. Exam shares are rough planning figures, so check the official exam outline for the current version.

Can you promise I will pass?

No. A planner helps you organise your time. It can’t guarantee any score.

What if it isn’t useful?

Email hello@thefocusflowlab.com within 14 days of buying and you’ll get a full refund, no questions asked.

Is there a free option?

Yes. The free study plan generators build a week-by-week schedule for each exam, and the free Study Focus Kit includes a printable study planner.
