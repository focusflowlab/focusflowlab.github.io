Who we are

FocusFlow Lab (thefocusflowlab.com) is run by Ahsan Raza in Lahore, Pakistan. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the website, the free study tools, the email list and the paid planners. It also covers a private video publishing tool, described further down.

The short version

  • You can use every study tool, plan and guide without giving us any personal information.
  • We only collect your email address if you sign up for the free Study Focus Kit, and only to send you the kit and FocusFlow Lab emails.
  • We don’t use cookies, ads or tracking across other sites, and we never sell your information.

Email list

When you sign up for the free Study Focus Kit, we collect your email address. Our email provider, MailerLite, also records technical details of the signup, such as the time and your IP address, as a record of your consent.

We use your address to send the kit, a short series of study emails, and occasional study tips and news about FocusFlow Lab planners. You confirm your address before we send anything. Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops all of them. MailerLite may also record whether emails are opened or links clicked, which helps us see which emails are useful.

Your address is stored with MailerLite, which processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. We don’t sell, rent or share it with anyone else. If you unsubscribe or don’t open our emails for a long time, we delete your address within 12 months. The signup form only contacts MailerLite when you press the button.

Purchases

Paid planners are sold through Gumroad, which acts as the merchant of record: it handles checkout, payment, tax and invoices under its own privacy policy. Your card details go directly to Gumroad and its payment processors. We never see or store them. We receive your name, email address, country and order details, which we use to deliver your download, handle refunds and answer support questions, and keep as long as needed for tax and accounting records.

Study tools and plans

The calculators, timer, flashcards, schedules and study plans run entirely in your browser. What you type, such as grades, exam dates or flashcards, is saved in your browser’s local storage so it’s there when you come back. It is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Analytics and cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are useful. It doesn’t set cookies, doesn’t track you across other sites and doesn’t identify individual visitors. The website itself sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which, like any web host, process technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site.

Videos and links

Some guides include a FocusFlow Lab YouTube video. The video only loads from YouTube (using its privacy-enhanced mode) after you press play; until then, nothing is sent to YouTube. Links to our social media profiles and to other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe at any time from any email. You can also ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it: email hello@thefocusflowlab.com and we’ll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, for example in the UK, the EU or California, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The tools can be used by anyone, but the email list and purchases are for people aged 16 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we’ll delete it.

Video publishing tool

This part of the policy covers the publishing tool that Ahsan Raza uses to post videos to his own channels, FocusFlow Lab and Wyrmheart Tales, on YouTube and TikTok. The tool is not offered to the public; it is used only by the channel owner.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok: after the account owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives the account’s basic profile (display name, username, avatar), the account’s posting settings (allowed privacy options, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are enabled, maximum video length), and permission to upload videos.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos, manage playlists and read basic channel statistics for the owner’s channels.

How it is used

This information is used only to show the account owner which account they are posting to and to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and confirmed. Nothing is posted without the owner’s explicit confirmation.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner’s own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or from your Google Account permissions page. Revoking access deletes the tool’s ability to use the stored tokens.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes affecting subscribers, we’ll also tell you by email.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: hello@thefocusflowlab.com
