How to use it

The planner spreads the pages you have left evenly across your reading days, then keeps the last two days for review if you want them. Rest days are skipped, so the daily number is what you really have to read.

  • Read once, properly. Close the book after each section and write down the main points from memory. The re-reading guide explains why this beats a second read.
  • Use the review days to recall, not re-read: skim your notes and test yourself on the main points.
  • Behind schedule? Put in the pages you’ve read so far and the plan resets from today.

Questions people ask

How are pages per day worked out?

Pages left ÷ reading days before the deadline, rounded up. Review days and rest days aren’t counted as reading days.

Does it count today?

Yes. The plan starts today and ends the day before your deadline.

Is my plan saved?

Yes, in this browser only, so you can come back and update the pages you’ve read.
