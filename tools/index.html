Which tool do you need?

Worried about grades? The weighted grade calculator shows where you stand in a course, the final grade calculator shows what you need on the final, and the GPA calculator shows how the semester changes your GPA.

Exams coming up? Start with the exam countdown to see your real study hours, then share them out with the study schedule maker. For one big exam, use a study plan for that exam instead.

Need it to stick? Make flashcards, plan reviews with the spaced review calendar, and get through set reading with the reading planner. Then sit down with the focus timer and do one block at a time.
