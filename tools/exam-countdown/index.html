How to use it

“Six weeks left” sounds like plenty. At three hours a day, five days a week, it’s 90 hours, and every topic, practice test and review day has to fit inside them.

  • Count hours, not days. Hours are what you spend. Days include work, classes and sleep.
  • Be honest about days a week. A plan that assumes seven study days usually breaks in week one.
  • Leave the last day free. Rest before an exam is part of the plan, not time lost.

Questions people ask

How is the number of study days worked out?

Days left times the days a week you study, divided by seven, rounded.

Does it count today?

It counts from today to the day before the exam.

Is my date saved?

Yes, in this browser only, so the countdown is there next time you open the page.
