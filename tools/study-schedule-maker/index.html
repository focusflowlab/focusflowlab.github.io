A study schedule for all your exams.

Add each subject and its exam date. You get hours per subject for every week.

Free, no sign-up. Works in your browser.

Your exams

SubjectExam dateWork needed

    Your study schedule

      Hours are rounded to half-hour blocks. Subjects drop off the schedule after their exam.

      How to use it

      The schedule shares out your daily hours among the subjects whose exams haven’t happened yet. Two things decide each subject’s share: how much work you said it needs, and how close its exam is. Nearer exams get more time, so you don’t run out of days on the first one.

      • Be honest about hours. Three real hours beat six planned ones.
      • Mark your hardest subjects as Heavy. They get about three times the time of a Light one.
      • Turn hours into blocks. Use the focus timer to work through each subject’s hours one block at a time.

      Questions people ask

      Is this a study timetable maker?

      Yes. It makes a printable week-by-week study timetable for all your subjects. Press “Print or save as PDF” to keep it.

      How does it decide hours per subject?

      Each study day, subjects with an upcoming exam share your hours. A subject’s share grows with the work it needs (Light, Normal, Heavy) and as its exam gets closer.

      Can I save or print it?

      Press “Print or save as PDF”. Your subjects and dates also stay in this browser for next time.

      What about big exams like the NCLEX or MCAT?

      For a single big exam, the exam-specific study plans are a better fit. They follow that exam’s content outline.
